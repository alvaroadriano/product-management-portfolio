# Enterprise Spend Management Platform — FARFETCH

**Role:** Procurement Lead, LATAM  
**Period:** April 2021–April 2023  
**Focus:** Enterprise platform implementation, procurement workflows, requirements, stakeholder alignment, and adoption

## Context

FARFETCH supported procurement operations across multiple markets and functions. The Coupa implementation work involved regional requirements in Brazil and Mexico and workflows shared across procurement, finance, and compliance stakeholders.

## The product challenge

The work required translating regional operating needs and control requirements into practical enterprise workflows. Requirements had to be understood across functions and aligned with a platform implementation, while supporting consistent processes and adoption.

## My contribution

As Procurement Lead for LATAM, I supported implementation work and represented regional procurement needs. My contribution included gathering and clarifying requirements, mapping business processes into platform workflows, coordinating with procurement, finance, compliance, and technical or functional partners, and supporting rollout and adoption activities.

The broader program included approval-flow design and integration requirements involving Coupa and connected enterprise processes. My role was business and product-facing; this case does not claim that I personally engineered integrations or owned the platform configuration.

## How I approached it

1. **Clarified regional needs:** brought Brazil and Mexico procurement requirements into implementation discussions.
2. **Mapped workflows:** helped translate purchasing and approval needs into clearer system and process requirements.
3. **Aligned stakeholders:** coordinated business, finance, and compliance perspectives so workflows could support operational needs and controls.
4. **Supported delivery and adoption:** worked with implementation partners through rollout activities and helped users transition to standardized workflows.

## Outcome and evidence

The work supported enterprise workflow implementation and process standardization across regional stakeholders. The available evidence supports a qualitative outcome; it does not provide a defensible adoption, savings, cycle-time, or compliance metric, so none is claimed here.

- Requirements discovery across functions and markets

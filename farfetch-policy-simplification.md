# Enterprise Policy Simplification — FARFETCH LATAM

**Role:** Procurement Lead, LATAM  
**Period:** 2021–2023  
**Focus:** Policy governance, cross-functional alignment, control environment, and process simplification

## Context

A broad set of regional policies needed review and alignment across business functions and control requirements. The work took place in a SOX context and involved stakeholders across LATAM as well as external specialists.

## Challenge

Over time, a large policy portfolio can become difficult to navigate. Related or outdated guidance can make it harder for teams to understand the current process, while control requirements still need to be covered. The goal was to review the portfolio carefully and simplify it without treating a smaller policy count as the only measure of success.

## My contribution

I coordinated a year-long review of approximately 200 LATAM policies, bringing together cross-functional stakeholders and external specialists. I helped organize review, discussion, and alignment across the portfolio; this was a collaborative effort, not sole authorship of every policy.

The review consolidated or eliminated policies where appropriate and also allowed new policies to be created where gaps were identified.

## Outcome and evidence

The overall policy count was reduced by 20% following the review. The figure reflects portfolio simplification through consolidation and removal while accounting for some new policies created during the process.

## What this demonstrates

- Cross-functional program coordination at regional scale
- Policy portfolio and process governance
- Balancing simplification with control requirements
- Working with external specialists and internal stakeholders
- Clear communication across a complex review effort

## What I learned

A smaller policy set is useful only when people can find and apply the right guidance. A sound review needs to look at duplication, gaps, ownership, and control needs together.
